What Your Chatbot Can Handle

Useful Conversations, Built Around Your Business

Your chatbot is shaped around the information customers need and the actions you want them to take.

Answer Common Questions

Give visitors clear answers about your services, pricing, timings, location, policies, and availability.

Capture Better Leads

Ask for the right details, such as name, phone number, service interest, and preferred time to connect.

Guide the Next Step

Direct visitors to a contact form, booking request, quotation enquiry, product page, or your team.

Stay Available After Hours

Help visitors outside business hours with useful first responses instead of leaving them waiting for a reply.

Stay Consistent

Make sure every visitor receives the same accurate, on-brand information your team has approved.

Hand Off When Needed

Route complex or sensitive conversations to a real person instead of forcing the visitor through an automated loop.
