Data Deletion Request

This page explains how to request deletion of personal information that Stack Automate may hold in connection with this website or an enquiry.

Effective date: September 12, 2026

1. How to submit a request

Send an email to info@stackautomates.com with the subject line “Data Deletion Request”. Please include enough information for us to identify your records, such as the email address or WhatsApp number used in your enquiry, and tell us what information you want deleted.

2. What we will do

We will review the request, verify that it comes from the person concerned or an authorised representative, and search the systems and service records we control. Requests sent to info@stackautomates.com will result in deletion of the relevant personal data within 48 hours after verification, unless a lawful exception requires us to retain specific information.

3. Verification and lawful exceptions

We may ask for reasonable additional information to protect against unauthorised deletion requests. We may retain limited information where necessary to comply with a legal obligation, establish or defend a legal claim, prevent fraud or abuse, maintain security records, complete a transaction, or document that a request was fulfilled. Retained information will be limited to what is necessary and protected appropriately.

4. Third-party platforms

Information held directly by WhatsApp, Meta, our hosting provider, messaging providers, or another independent platform may need to be deleted through that provider’s own controls and policies. We will take reasonable steps within our control and, where appropriate, explain how you can submit a corresponding request to the relevant provider.

5. Effect of deletion

Deletion may remove our ability to respond to an earlier enquiry, provide a requested service, maintain an account history, or recognise a previous preference. It does not affect information that has been irreversibly anonymised or information that we are legally permitted or required to keep.

6. Other privacy rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may also have rights to access, correct, restrict, object to, or receive a copy of your personal information, and to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. Contact info@stackautomates.com to exercise a right or ask a question. You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

7. Contact

For all deletion requests and privacy questions, email info@stackautomates.com. We will communicate the outcome of a verified request using the contact details provided.
