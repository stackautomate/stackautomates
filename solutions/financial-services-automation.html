Financial Services Features

Built for Advisors, Agents, and Lenders

Automation designed around the questions your clients ask most — loan status, policy details, account queries, and appointment scheduling.

Loan Status Updates

"Has my loan been approved?" answered instantly with the current stage — no need to call and wait on hold.

Policy Renewal Reminders

Automatic reminders before a policy expires, with renewal steps included — fewer lapsed policies, less follow-up work.

Appointment Scheduling

Clients book a consultation slot directly through chat, with automatic confirmation and reminders.

Document Checklist Sharing

"What documents do I need for a loan application?" gets an instant checklist, avoiding repeated back-and-forth.

EMI and Payment Reminders

Automatic reminders sent a few days before an EMI or premium is due, reducing missed payments and follow-up calls.

Secure Handoff to Your Team

For sensitive requests, the automation smoothly hands the chat to a real advisor — with full context, no repeating.
