Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Stack Automate may receive through this website and how we use it when you contact us or ask about our services.

Effective date: September 6, 2026

1. Who we are

Stack Automate provides practical WhatsApp automation, website chatbot development, and WhatsApp marketing services for businesses. In this policy, "Stack Automate," "we," "us," and "our" refer to Stack Automate.

2. Information you choose to share

When you use a contact form, request a demo, email us, or start a conversation, we may receive your name, business name, email address, phone number, industry, service interest, and the details in your message.

3. Information collected automatically

Our hosting provider or website tools may record basic technical details such as your IP address, browser, device type, referring page, and pages visited. We use this information to keep the website working, understand general usage, and investigate technical problems.

4. How we use information

  • To reply to enquiries and provide requested information.
  • To prepare proposals, demos, or service recommendations.
  • To deliver, maintain, and improve our website and services.
  • To protect the website, prevent misuse, and meet legal obligations.
  • To send service-related updates or marketing where permitted and appropriate.

5. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with providers that help us host the website, receive enquiries, schedule meetings, send messages, or deliver an agreed service. They may use it only to provide those services or as required by law.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

This website may use essential technologies for hosting, security, or basic functionality. Third-party services may set their own cookies or collect usage information under their own policies. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

7. Retention and security

We keep information only as long as reasonably needed for its purpose, business records, dispute resolution, or legal requirements. We use reasonable measures to protect it, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Your choices

You can ask what personal information we hold, request a correction, ask for deletion where applicable, or withdraw a marketing preference. Email info@stackautomates.com. Some information may need to be retained for legal or security reasons.

9. Third-party services and links

Our website may link to WhatsApp, social networks, and other third-party platforms. Those services operate under their own terms and privacy policies. We are not responsible for external websites or their privacy practices.

10. Children

Our services are intended for businesses and are not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through this website.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our website, services, or legal responsibilities change. The revised version will be posted here with a new effective date.

12. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your information can be sent to info@stackautomates.com.
